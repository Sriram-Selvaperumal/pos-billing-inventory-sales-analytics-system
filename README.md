# Open-Source POS and Billing Software with Inventory & Sales Reports

A complete **Point of Sale (POS) and Billing System** designed for retail businesses, featuring fast checkout, product and item management, inventory tracking, and detailed sales reporting. Built for stores aiming to replace manual billing with a reliable and efficient digital solution.

---

## Features

### Dashboard
- Today’s total sales
- Total customers count
- Sales trend graph (last 7 days)

### POS (Point of Sale)
- Add items to cart
- Real-time billing and total calculation
- Fast and simple checkout
- Automatic bill generation

### Items / Product Management
- Add, edit, update, and delete items (CRUD)
- Price, stock, and item details management

### Reports
- Today’s sales
- Weekly sales
- Monthly sales
- Complete transaction history

---

## Target Use Cases
This software is suitable for:
- Retail shops
- Supermarkets and grocery stores
- Restaurants and food outlets
- Pharmacies
- Wholesale and distribution businesses
- Small and medium enterprises
